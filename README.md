## About CCPAC

The **Chittagong College Physics & Astronomy Club (CCPAC)** is an academic community for students who are curious about physics, astronomy, mathematics, and the wider universe.

Established on **13 February 2025**, CCPAC creates opportunities to learn beyond the traditional curriculum through academic sessions, specialized camps, interactive events, science communication, and peer mentorship. Every question is welcome, and curiosity matters more than prior expertise.

> Don't just observe the universe. Explore it.

## Membership

Membership is open to **all Chittagong College students** interested in physics and astronomy. Whether you are just beginning your journey in science or already preparing for Olympiads, CCPAC is a space to learn, contribute, and grow.

Members can access:

- Telescope-assisted sky observation sessions
- Olympiad training and problem-solving guidance
- Academic sessions led by seniors and alumni
- Scientific writing and podcast opportunities
- An ECA certificate for members who maintain at least 75% attendance throughout the term
- A supportive community for higher-study guidance and collaboration
